Your password stays with you.

All three apps are open source, so you can read exactly what they do.

Mac iPhone Android

Stored in secure storage

Mac and iPhone keep it in the Keychain. Android encrypts it with a key held in Android Keystore. It’s never logged.

Used only to log in

On iPhone and Android it goes only to NYU Shanghai’s VPN server, after its certificate is checked. On Mac it goes only to Cisco Secure Client.

No automatic retries

Each login prompt is answered once. A wrong password fails instead of being tried again and again.
